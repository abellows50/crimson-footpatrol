Analyze a stretch of radio

Pulls every Pro EMS transmission in the range from OpenMHz a day at a time and runs it through the normal pipeline (the call board and Harvard matching). Clips already transcribed are reused, live traffic always goes first, and everything analyzed is kept, so results add up across runs. Expect roughly a few minutes per day of radio.

Harvard calls

Press Not Harvard on anything that isn't really a Harvard call: it leaves every Harvard count and chart, and its dispatch loses the Harvard tag. Press Harvard ✓ to confirm one (a confirmed “possible” call counts as Harvard), or open any dispatch below and press Mark as Harvard for one the matcher missed. Turn on “Show removed” to put one back.
